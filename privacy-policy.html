Fullpage Screenshot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Fullpage Screenshot is a Chrome extension that captures webpages and provides local tools for cropping, annotating, redacting, and exporting screenshots. This policy explains how the extension handles information.

Information the extension handles

When you choose to capture a page, the extension handles the visible website content contained in the screenshot. The extension does not access Chrome's browsing-history database or retain the page URL, page title, or capture time.

This information is processed and stored only on your device. We do not receive or collect it. The extension has no account system, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

How screenshots are processed

Screenshot capture, stitching, editing, and export happen locally in your browser. Screenshots, page content, and edits are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Local storage and retention

The extension temporarily places screenshot tiles in browser-managed storage so it can pass them from the capture process to the local editor. The editor deletes those temporary tiles as soon as it assembles the screenshot. The assembled screenshot and edits then remain only in the editor tab's memory until you close or reload that tab.

If a capture is canceled, fails, or cannot open the editor, the extension deletes its temporary tiles. Redaction covers pixels in exported files; the editor's in-memory original remains available only while that editor tab is open.

Exported PNG and PDF files are saved through your browser and remain wherever you choose to download them.

Chrome permissions

Fullpage Screenshot requests only the permissions needed for its core features:

  • activeTab: Accesses the tab you choose only after you click the extension icon.
  • scripting: Measures and scrolls the selected page while taking the screenshot, then restores the page's scroll position.

The extension does not request storage, unlimited storage, persistent access to every website, Chrome debugger access, or access to your browsing history.

Sharing and selling data

We do not receive, sell, rent, share, or transfer user data because the extension does not transmit user data to us. The extension does not use remote services or remotely hosted code.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children’s privacy

Fullpage Screenshot does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data handling changes, this policy will be updated before the changed behavior is released. The revision date at the top will indicate when the policy was last updated.
