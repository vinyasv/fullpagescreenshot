# Chrome Web Store beta listing draft

**Name:** Fullpage Screenshot

**Short description:** Capture complete webpages, crop and annotate free, and export PNG or multi-page PDF. Screenshots stay on your device.

**Description:** Click the toolbar icon to capture the current webpage. Fullpage Screenshot shows progress while it scrolls through the page and assembles a full-resolution screenshot. Move, edit, recolor, or delete annotations; crop the result; and export PNG or an image-based PDF sized for A4 or Letter. Use Recapture to capture the page again. No account, watermark, paywall, analytics, or upload.

**Permission explanation:** `activeTab` and `scripting` scroll the chosen tab after the user clicks the icon. `storage` and `unlimitedStorage` hold the image locally for editing. The extension does not request debugger or persistent host permissions.

**Data-use disclosure:** Screenshots, URLs, and edits are processed locally; no data is collected or transmitted. Captures persist in Chrome's local extension storage until discarded or the extension is removed. Publish `PRIVACY.md` at a public URL and use that URL in the dashboard.

**Beta distribution:** Private or unlisted. Add “BETA” to the beta listing name and state that it is for testing, per Chrome's distribution instructions. Prepare at least one screenshot of the editor, icons, support contact, and a verified publisher account before submission.
