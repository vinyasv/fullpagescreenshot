# Fullpage Screenshot privacy policy

Fullpage Screenshot captures only the browser tab you select by clicking its toolbar icon. The extension processes screenshots and edits on your device. It does not transmit screenshots, URLs, page content, or annotations to us or any third party. It has no account, analytics, advertising, or remote service.

The extension stores screenshot data in Chrome's extension-local storage so its editor can open and export the capture. Captures persist after closing the editor or restarting Chrome until you select **Discard** or uninstall the extension. A successful **Recapture** deletes the capture it replaces. Redaction covers pixels in exported files; the original screenshot remains in local storage until deleted. Exported files are saved wherever your browser downloads them and are under your control.

Fullpage Screenshot uses `activeTab` and `scripting` to scroll the tab you choose after you click its icon. `storage` and `unlimitedStorage` hold local capture data. It does not request debugger access or persistent host access, and it does not use remotely hosted code.

For questions or deletion of locally stored captures, use the editor's **Discard** button or remove the extension. This policy must be hosted at a public URL before Chrome Web Store submission.
